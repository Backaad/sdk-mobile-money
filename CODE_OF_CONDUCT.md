# Code of conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short:

- Be welcoming and respectful, whatever someone's experience, background,
  language or identity. Questions from beginners are welcome, in English or
  French.
- Discuss ideas, not people. Give and accept constructive criticism, and admit
  your own mistakes.
- Harassment, insults, sexualized language or images, personal attacks,
  publishing someone's private information, and any other conduct that would be
  inappropriate in a professional setting are not accepted.

It applies in every space of the project (issues, pull requests, discussions)
and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer,
[@Backaad](https://github.com/Backaad), through the contact shown on their
GitHub profile. You can also report content to GitHub with **Report content**
or **Report abuse**. Reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainer may remove comments, commits and other contributions that break
this code, and may warn, suspend or ban a participant, following the
enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
